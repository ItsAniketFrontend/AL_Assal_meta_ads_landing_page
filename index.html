Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver across the UAE?

Yes. We supply and deliver to Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi and across all the Emirates, for projects of every size.

Can I see the actual slabs before I buy?

Absolutely. Our 33,000 m² stockyard in Sajja, Sharjah is open for visits, where you can pick the exact slab for your project. Prefer to stay remote? We send samples and detailed photos.

Do you offer custom cutting and finishing?

Yes, we handle cutting to size, edge profiles and a wide range of surface finishes to match your specification.

Do you supply to contractors, architects and developers?

We do. We work directly with architects, designers, contractors and developers, with project pricing and support for large-scale, long-term jobs.

How quickly will I get a quote?

Share your requirements and we'll respond within 24 hours with a clear, competitive quote.

Which stones do you stock?

Quartz, Travertine, Quartzite, Onyx, Limestone, Sandstone and Porcelain, plus marble, granite and rare exotics. Over 400 varieties in total.
